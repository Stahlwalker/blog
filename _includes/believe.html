What is Stahlwalker?

Stahlwalker is a place for building and fixing things on the web.

I started it to focus on the technical side of frontend work, debugging, and understanding how systems behave once they're in use. Over time, my work shifted from coordinating projects to writing code, tracing issues, and owning implementation details end to end.

The work here focuses on frontend development, debugging, and brand foundations. Practical problems, real constraints, and solutions meant to hold up over time.
